Frunk Filter Swap

A bit more involved than the cabin filters — ten Torx screws and four filter pieces, but still nothing beyond basic hand tools.

DIY / Maintenance Updated August 2026 5–min read

The frunk filter system sits up front, mounted above the frunk bin near the engine bay, and pre-filters outside air before it reaches the cabin. It's a bit more time-consuming than the cabin filter swap simply because of screw count — ten Torx 20 screws instead of one — but every step is straightforward with basic hand tools. A power driver with a Torx 20 bit will save you a good bit of time over doing all ten by hand.

Materials Required

  • T20 Torx Screwdriver (or Torx 20 power driver bit)View on Amazon →
  • Frunk FiltersView on Amazon →

The Process

  1. Remove the Top Panel

    Remove the top panel covering the frunk filter housing. It's held on by a handful of plastic clips that stay attached to the cover itself rather than the car, so there's nothing loose to lose track of. Start on one side and lift gradually until you feel the clips pop free, then work your way across. Set the cover aside somewhere safe.

  2. Remove the Filter Cover Screws

    Remove the 10 Torx 20 screws holding the filter cover panel in place. There's one screw on its own on the passenger side, with the rest split between above and below the rubber seal. Take your time here — if a screw drops down into the housing, it's a genuine hassle to fish back out, and it will not be a good day. Once all 10 are out, the filter cover lifts away; set it aside along with the screws so nothing gets lost.

  3. Swap the Filters

    This one's simple — remove all four existing filter pieces and swap in the new ones. They're not side-specific, so either filter set can go on either side. Just make sure the actual filter element ends up on top of the backing piece, not underneath it.

  4. Reassemble

    Reinstall the cover and all 10 Torx 20 screws in reverse order. Align the top panel back into place and press down along both sides until the clips seat firmly.
