Tesla FAQs

Quick, straight answers to the questions Model Y owners ask most — settings, quirks, and the stuff that isn't obvious from the manual.

L5 Info Booth

Most of these live in one place: tap the car icon in the bottom-left corner of the touchscreen to open Controls, then drill into the sections below (Charging, Safety, Dynamics, etc). A few settings live under Climate or the Tesla app instead — those are called out in the table.

Highest-Impact Settings

If you only change five things, make it these: keep your daily charge limit around 80%, enable scheduled departure with preconditioning, turn Sentry Mode off at trusted locations, turn off Cabin Overheat Protection unless you need it, and leave the car plugged in whenever it's practical.

Setting How to Get There Recommended Value Why It Helps
Charge Limit (Daily) Controls → Charging 80% (or the car's suggestion) Reduces long-term degradation while leaving plenty of daily range. Save 100% for longer trips.
Scheduled Charging / Departure Controls → Charging → Schedule Set to your normal departure time Lets the battery finish charging and precondition right before you leave.
Precondition While Plugged In Charging or Climate → Schedule Enabled Warms/cools the battery and cabin using wall power instead of the battery.
Sentry Mode Controls → Safety → Sentry Mode Off at Home/Work One of the biggest sources of parked battery drain.
Cabin Overheat Protection Controls → Safety → Cabin Overheat Protection Off (or No A/C) Reduces battery use while parked in hot weather — this is a comfort feature, not a battery-protection one.
Acceleration Mode Controls → Dynamics Chill (optional) Gentler acceleration can modestly improve efficiency.
Climate Temperature Climate controls 68–72°F (20–22°C) Moderate HVAC settings stretch range further.
Keep Climate On / Camp / Dog Mode Climate controls Use only when needed Continuous HVAC while parked adds up fast.
Summon Standby Controls → Autopilot Off unless actively used Reduces standby energy use.
Navigate to Supercharger Navigation Always route there via Nav Automatically preconditions the battery for faster DC charging.
Leave Vehicle Plugged In Charging habit Plug in whenever practical Helps the car maintain battery temperature efficiently.
Check the Tesla App Less Usage habit Avoid unnecessary wake-ups Opening the app wakes the car and adds standby drain.
Tire Pressure Service → Tire Pressure Maintain recommended PSI Properly inflated tires roll more efficiently.

Use the in-car Energy app to see how these settings actually affect your real-world consumption over time.
